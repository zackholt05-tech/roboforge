## 5. Engineering incompleteness and reruns

docs/FINAL_TEST_FREEZE.md §4: an evaluation with no summary, fewer than all starts, a failed verify_record audit or a non-canonical platform is engineering-incomplete; it is rerun once only for a fault outside the policy before its summary is written, under a recorded decision (attempt 2); still incomplete, its family is ENGINEERING-INCOMPLETE, left out of the bootstrap (the draw order skips it), and its contrasts are not computed. Episodes labelled engineering_error count as failures and are reported separately.
